EMAIL CAMPAIGN // PRODUCT LAUNCH

Freeze Pipe
Welcome Email

Strategy & Context

The Challenge

Smokers are inherently skeptical of novelty hardware. Most assume a freezable glycerin coil is just an overhyped ice pinch gimmick that clogs or shatters after two weeks. Proving cold-filtered thermodynamics through a phone screen requires hard physics, not marketing fluff. We needed to validate the cooling claim instantly, lower the barrier to entry with a free-piece giveaway, and categorize a dizzying catalog of bongs and bubblers into straightforward routines before new subscribers closed the tab.

Objective & Vision

The Goal

Turn a standard welcome sequence into an interactive buyers' playbook. We anchored the layout around an undeniable proof point, cooling smoke by up to 300 degrees before it hits throat or lungs. Instead of pushing generic catalog dumps, the email splits readers into distinct usage personas, from everyday quick-hitters to weekend heavyweights looking for glass centerpieces. High-contrast cards and frictionless raffle entries keep engagement high, walking hesitant newcomers from curious lurkers to confident first-time owners.

6 Core Pillars

Design Highlights

Hierarchy

Mobile First

Built exclusively for single-thumb scrolling. The entire vertical spine stacks thermographic proof metrics, giveaway steps, and glassware options in an unbroken rhythm that never pinches or truncates on mobile clients.

Brand Credibility

Trust-Led Design

Precision borosilicate glass deserves serious editorial restraint. Crisp contrast, surgical negative space, and deep neutral backgrounds elevate the hardware into a laboratory-grade tool rather than a novelty headshop curiosity.

Information Architecture

Functional Clarity

Big numbers kill doubt. Prominent callouts and clean vector schematics explain exactly how non-freezing glycerin coils chill smoke, translating complex thermal claims into obvious common sense within seconds.

Compatibility

Responsive Rendering

Engineered defensively against messy inbox engines. Stacked persona blocks and glow accents hold tight margins without collapsing across Apple Mail, Gmail dark mode, or Outlook desktop.

Conversion

Segmented CTA

Choice paralysis kills revenue. Instead of throwing twenty bongs on screen, tailored calls to action split shoppers by smoking habits, guiding both discreet bubbler users and heavy rig buyers directly to their sweet spot.

Performance

Optimized Performance

High-resolution glass reflections and frozen coil bubbles compressed for instantaneous loads. Zero clipping, no choppy scroll lag, and featherweight file footprints that sail right past Gmail clip limits.

Project 03 of 03 Email Design
EMAIL CAMPAIGN // PRODUCT LAUNCH

Freeze Pipe
Welcome Email

Strategy & Context

The Challenge

The biggest challenge was overcoming the consumer misconception that a glycerin coil doesn't cool smoke better than an ice pinch. Since its benefits are hard to see and feel, the design had to demonstrate its superiority as a fun trick and a real improvement, while also being easy to use in busy areas. I created a mobile-first experience to address technical doubts, encourage purchases with an automated raffle, and organize products into "user personas" to help new subscribers decide.

Objective & Vision

The Goal

I designed an educational tool showcasing our technical expertise. Bold, contrasting fonts highlighted key data points like "Cools Air By Up To 300°" to emphasize our effectiveness. A clear visual structure made the "Free Piece" raffle easy to join, and a story about lifestyle choices (e.g., "Quick & Easy" vs. "Apex Predator") helped customers choose based on their smoking habits. A stacked-card interface with big, easy-to-click buttons ensured a smooth transition from skepticism to shopping on mobile. This email became a helpful shopping assistant that encourages purchases.

6 Core Pillars

Design Highlights

01 / Hierarchy

Mobile First

Optimized with a fluid, single-column layout for seamless scrolling on mobile devices. Prioritizing vertical hierarchy, the technical "cooling" proof and product categories are easily digestible for users on the go.

02 / Brand Credibility

Trust-Led Design

Using clean negative space and high-contrast typography, I created a visual "prestige" that builds credibility and dismantles skepticism. This presentation positions the product as a high-end engineering feat rather than a gimmick.

03 / Information Architecture

Functional Clarity

By using clear icons and data-driven callouts (like the "300°" cooling stat), I simplified complex product specs into easy-to-understand visuals. This layout clearly explains the "how it works" aspect, effectively converting doubters.

04 / Compatibility

Responsive Rendering

This design ensures that sophisticated elements like durable product glass and stacked category cards remain pixel-perfect across major email clients, from Apple Mail to Gmail.

05 / Conversion

Segmented CTA

I used a strategic "category narrative" to help users self-identify their needs. High-contrast buttons for personas like "Quick & Easy" or "Apex Predator" reduced choice paralysis and streamlined the path from inbox to checkout.

06 / Performance

Optimized Performance

High-quality, compressed product photography ensures detailed glass textures and vibrant glycerin coils load instantly, minimizing friction and keeping users engaged with the visual storytelling.

Guard Pro

Guard Pro was engineered to redefine table tennis equipment and performance gear for tournament athletes. Blending aerodynamic precision with high-impact defensive shielding, the brand bridges the gap between competitive agility and industrial-grade protection.

Brand Identity & Typography

At the core of the identity is the interlocking paddle-shield monogram, a dynamic glyph calibrated for both defensive tenacity and rapid forward attack. Backed by rigorous contrast standards and a custom athletic display typeface, the system thrives in high-speed, broadcast-lit arena environments.

Tactical Tournament Performance

From tournament carbon paddles to arena-scale visual architecture, GuardPro's identity brings championship focus to life through physical materials, tactile grip textures, and razor-sharp typographic execution. Every curve and seal embodies the mantra: Play Like a Pro.

The chromatic hierarchy of GuardPro is anchored by Guardian Blue (#0056B3) for steadfast authority and Trust Navy (#1A3263) for structural depth, energized by high-visibility Energy Serve (#A3E723) highlights that track ball trajectories in high-speed play.

The bespoke Active Uno typeface features forward-angled diagonals, squared internal counters, and balanced terminal weights engineered to stay razor-sharp whether micro-embossed onto rubber paddle edging or projected across arena scoreboard displays.

United by the signature “Play Like a Pro” tournament seal and interlocking paddle-shield geometry, GuardPro delivers an uncompromising visual presence for modern racket sports.

Project 02 of 03 Email Design
EMAIL CAMPAIGN // PRODUCT LAUNCH

iPhone 16e
Email Campaign

Strategy & Context

The Challenge

The challenge was translating a premium, high-tech product launch into an email inbox. Designing for Apple requires a "prestige" aesthetic, where white space is crucial. I had to present six technical specifications (Display, Chip, Camera, etc.) without overwhelming the recipient or causing abandonment.

Objective & Vision

The Goal

My goal was to create an editorial Visual Specification Map that introduces Apple Intelligence as a "hero" hook. A balanced grid system allows users to scan for their most important features. Success is defined by a smooth transition from brand awareness ("Introducing") to direct action ("Learn More" and "Pre-Order").

6 Core Pillars

Design Highlights

01 / Hierarchy

Mobile First

Optimized with a fluid, single-column layout to ensure a seamless reading experience on mobile devices, where most email engagement takes place.

02 / Aesthetic

Brand Consistency

Mirrors Apple's signature minimalist style by cleverly using negative space and using high-quality typography to give off a professional vibe.

03 / Information Architecture

Visual Readability

By focusing on high-contrast ratios and a clear grid system, we make those tricky technical details easy to understand at a quick glance.

04 / Compatibility

Responsive Rendering

Engineered for universal compatibility, this design ensures that sophisticated elements like soft shadows and custom grids remain pixel-perfect across all major email clients.

05 / Conversion

Focused CTA

By using high-contrast buttons in strategic spots, we're helping users easily move from what they're interested in to taking action, which really boosts traffic to the site!

06 / Performance

Fast Loading

By using high-quality, compressed files, we can make sure your site loads quickly, so you won't lose any visitors, all while keeping the visuals top-notch.

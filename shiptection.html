Project 09 of 12 Web Design

ShipTection Website Revamp

A conversion-focused UI/UX redesign and modular design system engineered to turn post-purchase shipping friction into an authoritative B2B revenue driver for Shopify merchants.

01 / STRATEGIC FOUNDATIONS

From Friction to Conversion

Aligning merchant acquisition incentives with seamless customer protection to drive measurable pipeline growth.

The Challenge

Low Trust & Conversion Drop-Off

The original platform suffered from an unfocused value proposition and fragmented narrative flow. Without clear B2B conversion pathways or prominent calls to action, Shopify merchants struggled to understand the platform's core financial upside: turning post-purchase shipping friction into automated revenue. Uncalibrated visual assets and dense copy created cognitive fatigue, driving high bounce rates and stalling qualified merchant demo bookings.

The Solution

Brand Authority & Conversion Flow

Engineered a high-fidelity B2B web experience grounded in speed, credibility, and conversion ergonomics. Replaced low-resolution graphics with crisp vector iconography, established a modular typographic hierarchy, and integrated persistent conversion anchors across the sticky header and hero fold. An interactive 5-step onboarding walkthrough and prominent merchant social proof guide users seamlessly toward demo bookings and app installations.

02 / COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS

Before & After Exhibition

A direct contrast highlighting structural improvements, cognitive clarity, and conversion mechanics between the legacy platform and the redesigned experience.

Before // Legacy Site Cluttered & Passive

Identified Friction Points

  • Vague Positioning: "Shipping Issues?" framed the service as a reactive consumer problem rather than a merchant revenue driver.
  • Missing CTAs: Zero calls to action in the header or hero fold left visitors without a clear path forward.
  • Zero Social Proof: No merchant partner logos or trust credentials above the fold to validate adoption.
  • Visual Clutter: Uncalibrated device graphics and fragmented imagery weakened overall brand credibility.
After // Redesigned Site Focused & Action-Driven

Engineered UX Solutions

  • Targeted B2B Value Prop: Authoritative headline speaking directly to modern e-commerce merchants.
  • Persistent Dual CTAs: High-visibility "Book A Demo" and "How It Works" anchors in the header and hero.
  • Instant Social Proof: Dedicated "Trusted By" marquee showcasing validated Shopify merchant brands.
  • 5-Step Guided Onboarding: Interactive progressive tabs clearly mapping app installation to automated claims.
03 / INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

Blueprint & User Journey Mapping

Establishing intuitive navigation architecture, persona segregation, and conversion pathways across six core site destinations before high-fidelity visual styling.

01 / Persona Segregation

Dual Audience Pathways

Separated merchant acquisition flows from end-consumer claims processing. Merchants get immediate ROI data and demo funnels; shoppers receive a frictionless claim filing interface without cluttering the B2B marketing journey.

02 / Persistent Wayfinding

Sticky Navigation Core

Engineered a fixed header and anchored sub-navigation that keeps essential touchpoints (Shopify App installation, merchant portal, claim filing, and demo requests) accessible across all scroll depths.

03 / Modular Taxonomy

Scalable Component Blocks

Standardized reusable content modules across the homepage and merchant landing pages, including dynamic revenue share calculators, 3-pillar benefit cards, and searchable FAQ accordions.

04 / DESIGN RATIONALE

Why It Works: The 6 Conversion Pillars

Strategic UI and UX principles engineered to reduce cognitive friction, establish instant merchant authority, and accelerate app adoption.

Pillar 01

Strategic CTA Placement

"Book A Demo" and "How It Works" actions are persistently visible across the sticky navbar and hero fold, ensuring merchants can initiate onboarding the exact second purchase intent peaks.

Pillar 02

Sticky Navigation Core

The fixed glass header keeps essential tools at arm's reach. Direct Shopify app store access, merchant claims management, and demo scheduling remain fluid across the entire browsing journey.

Pillar 03

Scannable Hierarchy

High-contrast display typography paired with structured section taxonomies ("Why ShipTection?", "How It Works") allows time-constrained e-commerce directors to digest core value in under 5 seconds.

Pillar 04

Intentional Negative Space

A balanced purple-on-neutral palette and generous whitespace eliminate cognitive fatigue, allowing key revenue numbers, partner badges, and primary action buttons to stand out without distraction.

Pillar 05

Progressive Flow & Focus

Interactive tabbed walkthroughs for onboarding and transparent side-by-side merchant pricing keep users immersed on a single canvas, eliminating tedious multi-page navigation.

Pillar 06

Mobile-First Parity

Engineered from the ground up for mobile viewports. Thumb-accessible touch targets, responsive drawer navigation, and clean vertical stacking ensure a frictionless experience on handheld devices.

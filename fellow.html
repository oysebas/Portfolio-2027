Project 01 of 03 Email Design
EMAIL CAMPAIGN // PRODUCT LAUNCH

Fellow Product
Email Campaign

Strategy & Context

The Challenge

The challenge was translating Fellow's design-forward, boutique aesthetic into a conversion-focused email experience. High-end coffee gear relies heavily on tactile appeal and minimalist craft, where breathing room is crucial. I had to showcase multiple hardware features (PID temp control, gooseneck flow rate, counterbalance grip) and customizable finishes without crowding the layout or causing recipient drop-off.

Objective & Vision

The Goal

My goal was to create an editorial Brew Bar Feature Map that introduces the Stagg EKG Pro as an elevated "morning ritual" hook. A modular tier system allows coffee enthusiasts to scan precision technical specs, preview countertop finishes, and explore complementary gear seamlessly. Success is defined by a smooth transition from lifestyle awareness ("Master the Pour") to direct action ("Upgrade Your Setup" and "Customize & Order").

6 Core Pillars

Design Highlights

01 / Hierarchy

Mobile First

Optimized with a fluid, vertical column hierarchy to ensure a seamless reading and tapping experience on mobile screens, where morning email checks take place.

02 / Aesthetic

Brand Consistency

Mirrors Fellow's signature boutique aesthetic by pairing warm oatmeal canvas neutrals with obsidian contrast and generous white space to give off a modern kitchen vibe.

03 / Information Architecture

Visual Readability

By pairing custom minimalist line iconography with an organized 3-column spec matrix, we make nuanced brewing mechanics effortless to digest at a quick glance.

04 / Interaction

Responsive Rendering

Features an interactive colorway swatch component that lets users preview custom hardware finishes directly within the email before navigating to checkout.

05 / Conversion

Focused CTA

By placing high-contrast action buttons at natural intent moments, we guide the user smoothly from hero discovery through social proof directly to conversion.

06 / Performance

Fast Loading

By using retina-ready, compressed asset exports, the layout renders instantly and remains pixel-perfect across Apple Mail, Gmail, and Outlook clients.
